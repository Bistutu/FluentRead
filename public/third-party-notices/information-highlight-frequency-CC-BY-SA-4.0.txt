The common Chinese character table and the common English word table are
derived from the word frequency data of wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq). The wordfreq code is licensed under
Apache License 2.0; its data is redistributable under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). The upstream sources of that
data and their own notices are listed in the wordfreq README, section
"License": https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq#license
Changes made: Chinese word frequencies were summed per character and only the
rank order of the 2,500 most frequent characters is kept; for English only the
rank order of the 2,000 most frequent alphabetic words is kept.

The traditional-to-simplified character pairs were produced with
opencc-python-reimplemented 0.1.7 (https://github.com/yichen0831/opencc-python),
which packages the conversion dictionaries of OpenCC
(https://github.com/BYVoid/OpenCC), licensed under Apache License 2.0.
